Enrollment

Our Step-by-Step Admission Process

We welcome you to enroll your child into Cornerstone Group Of Schools. Follow this simple path to secure admission.

1

Purchase Form

Purchase the application form from our administrative office on campus or apply online via our secure admissions portal below.

2

Entrance Exams

Your child will take entrance tests covering Mathematics, English, and general reasoning at our designated testing blocks.

3

Oral Interview

A parent and child interactive interview with the Director of Schools or Principal follows to review child behavior and academic standing.

4

Admission Offer

Successful candidates receive admission letters. Secure registration by making tuition payments at our bursar-approved bank details.

Entry Requirements

Please ensure you bring along the following documents during the interview stage:

Preschool & Nursery

  • A copy of child's birth certificate
  • Immunisation record card
  • Two recent passport photographs

Primary & Secondary Level

  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • Last academic report sheet (Report Card)
  • Four recent passport photographs
  • Satisfactory score in our entrance examination

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When do admission registrations open?

Registration for each academic year commences in January and closes in August. Entrance examinations are held in blocks from April to July.

Do you offer primary boarding school?

No. Our boarding house facilities are strictly reserved for secondary school students (JSS 1 – SSS 3) to ensure appropriate maturity, safety, and focused studying.

Are midterm transfers accepted?

Yes. Depending on seat availability and child performance in our mid-term assessment tests, we accept student transfers at the start of second or third terms.

What sports are available at boarding school?

Boarders participate in football training, basketball, athletics practice, table tennis tournaments, and group Christian fellowships every weekend.

School Portal Access

Our online portal allows students, parents, and teachers to access term results, download fees invoices, pay tuition bills online, and view weekly learning timetables from any device.

If you have registered, use your student registration ID or parent email address to sign in. For registration issues, kindly mail support@cornerstonecollege.com.
