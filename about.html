Our Story

Almost 20 Years of Providing Quality Education

Founded in 2008, Cornerstone Group of Schools was established with a singular mission: to provide a balanced primary and secondary educational program that integrates rigorous academic training with deep moral values. Located in the tranquil, serene environs of New Iyaji Layout in Oyo State, Nigeria, our school has expanded from a small block of classrooms to a sprawling co-educational campus offering both day-school and high-standard boarding options.

Over the years, we have earned a stellar reputation for scholastic integrity. We believe that true education should develop the total child—intellectually, physically, and spiritually. Cornerstone graduates proceed to universities well-prepared, carrying forward our heritage of strict personal discipline and outstanding merit.

Our Vision Statement

To be a premier Christian model institution of learning that raises highly disciplined, creative, and competent leaders who excel academically, morally, and spiritually, making a lasting impact in a globalized society.

Our Mission Statement

We are dedicated to providing a comprehensive, technology-driven education built on solid biblical values. Guided by qualified, caring educators, we create a secure and conducive environment that stimulates critical thinking, practical research, and ethical responsibility in every child.
