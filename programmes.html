Early Years

Preschool & Nursery (Ages 18 Months – 5 Years)

Our early childhood programme focuses on cognitive, sensory, and spiritual foundations. In a warm, loving, and highly supervised environment, children take their first steps in numeracy, literacy, and community interaction through play-based activities and bible stories.

  • Early Reading and Phonics (letter sounds & word formations)
  • Sensory coordination and creative hand-eye activities
  • Basic Numbers, counting, and simple mathematical reasoning
  • Character foundation and introduction to Christian values

Foundational Years

Primary School (Basic 1 – 6)

Our primary curriculum blends academic rigor with initial critical thinking skills. Under the guidance of skilled educators, pupils build confidence, learn primary sciences, study humanities, and acquire initial digital computing skills.

  • Core Mathematics, Quantitative reasoning, and Word Problems
  • English Grammar, Reading Comprehension, and Creative Writing
  • Basic Science, Technology, and Agricultural Studies
  • Social Studies, Civic Education, and Bible Knowledge studies
  • Introduction to Desktop Computing operations
Enroll in Primary School →
High School Years

Secondary School (JSS 1 – SSS 3)

Our secondary department provides a comprehensive education preparing students for national and international examinations. Blending a rigorous academic syllabus with STEM computing and boarding house discipline, we raise exam-ready scholars and mature young adults.

  • Junior Secondary: Mathematics, English, Integrated Science, Business Studies, Yoruba Language, French, Computer Studies.
  • Senior Science: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Geography, Agricultural Science.
  • Senior Arts & Commercial: Government, Literature in English, Financial Accounting, Commerce, Christian Religious Studies.
  • Rigorous test drills for WAEC, NECO, and UTME (JAMB)
